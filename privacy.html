Privacy policy - Pi Drive

Pi Drive is a private tool used only by its owner, Tim Rooijers. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive to move files from one folder to his home media server.

No data is shared with, sold to or processed for anyone else. Google user data is only used for this purpose, stays on the owner's own devices, and is not used for advertising or any other purpose.
